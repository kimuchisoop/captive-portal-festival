School Festival Guest Wi-Fi — provisional local development terms

This shared network is intended for visitors' Internet access. Speed and continuous availability are not guaranteed. Service may be limited, interrupted or ended due to congestion or equipment conditions.
Do not disrupt other users or equipment, access systems without authorization, or use the service unlawfully. Keep your device up to date and take care when handling sensitive information.
The service records the consent time, terms version, language and a connection reference for operation. The official retention period and contact point must be announced before production use.
